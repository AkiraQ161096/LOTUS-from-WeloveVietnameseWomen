Biophysical Foundations

How LOTUS Works: Biophysical Workflow

A systematic four-step bridge connecting cellular electrophysiology to actionable, plain-language patient early warning.

01

Attach & Couple

Flexible serpentine hydrogel conforms directly to the breast epidermis. The ionic matrix hydrates the stratum corneum, reducing skin-contact impedance below 20 kΩ without abrasive prep.

Contact Z: < 20 kΩ
02

Bilateral Measure

AD5940 AFE injects a non-invasive AC sweep (1–100 kHz, <80 µA capped) across tetrapolar electrodes. Simultaneously measures surface potential offset (ΔV) and bioimpedance Z(f).

Sweep: 56.23 kHz β-disp
03

AI Feature Fusion

On-device Bi-LSTM & Random Forest compare contralateral breast quadrants, extracting Cole-Cole parameters (τ, α) and Contralateral Asymmetry Ratio (CAR), filtering menstrual drift.

CAR = |Z_L - Z_R| / Z_avg
04

Understand & Act

In under 5 seconds, the user sees a plain-language tri-level status. If persistent asymmetry is confirmed, the app generates a clinical summary report for hospital oncology consultation.

Laboratory Verification

3-Layer Breast Tissue Mimicking Gelatin Phantom

To experimentally evaluate biophysical impedance drop (ΔZ) and surface potential variations (ΔV) prior to in-vivo trials, LOTUS was verified on a synthetic anatomical breast phantom (Gómez Cortés et al., 2024; Karpov et al., 2025).

LAYER 01 — 5 to 8 mm

Superficial Adipose Layer

Formulated from Gelatin, Glycerol, vegetable oil emulsion, and deionized water. Accurately simulates the low electrical conductivity of breast fat tissue: σ ≈ 0.02 – 0.04 S/m.

LAYER 02 — 20 to 30 mm

Deep Fibroglandular Layer

Synthesized from high-bloom gelatin and 0.9% physiological NaCl solution to calibrate healthy glandular tissue conductivity: σ ≈ 0.4 – 0.5 S/m.

INCLUSIONS — 5, 10, 15 mm

Proliferative Lesion Inclusions

Spherical hydrogel nodes with elevated NaCl/KCl and water density modeling depolarized proliferative clusters: σ ≈ 0.8 – 1.2 S/m, successfully producing detectable ΔZ impedance drops.
